# Dogovoroff visual identity

## Style Prompt

Premium northern minimalism for a high-trust legal practice: disciplined black space, pearl-white typography, precise structural lines and one restrained glacial accent. The hero motion feels like a slow passage through monumental Siberian ice—quiet, tactile and confident rather than decorative or fantastical.

## Colors

- `#050505` — primary black canvas
- `#0B0C0D` — softened black for depth
- `#FAFAF7` — warm pearl white
- `#A4A6A7` — restrained silver
- `#8FB9CA` — glacial accent, used sparingly

## Typography

- Website interface: Manrope, existing weights and spacing
- Editorial accent: Cormorant Garamond italic, existing usage only
- Northern motion asset: no embedded typography; the animation remains purely atmospheric

## Motion

- Slow cinematic movement with a 10–12 second breathing rhythm
- Sine-based in/out movement for ambient layers; no bounce or elastic easing
- The first and last frames must match for a seamless loop
- Prefer depth, refraction and light movement over obvious zooming
- Reduced-motion users receive the static poster without autoplay

## What NOT to Do

- No bright neon, saturated aurora gradients or fantasy-cave imagery
- No fast camera moves, flashes, jump cuts or dense particle storms
- No generic stock Arctic landscapes, people, animals or buildings
- No added interface copy, badges or promotional typography inside the artwork
- No runtime WebGL dependency for the hero

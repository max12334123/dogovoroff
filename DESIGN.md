# Dogovoroff homepage visual identity

## Style Prompt

Premium cinematic LegalTech for a high-trust legal practice: disciplined blue-black space, pearl-white editorial typography, precise structural lines, black chrome and one restrained glacial accent. The northern reference is present in the horizon and material, never as decorative folklore. This brief applies to the public homepage; the protected cabinet and staff workspace have their own design and security boundaries.

## Colors

- `#05080C` — primary blue-black canvas
- `#0A1017` — softened panel for depth
- `#F4F5F1` — warm pearl white
- `#A5B0B7` — restrained silver
- `#ABCDDA` — glacial accent, used sparingly

## Typography

- Homepage display: Geologica Variable at light weights (150–270), with large-scale editorial hierarchy
- Homepage interface: Geologica Variable for precise, contemporary LegalTech labels and body copy
- Other routes retain Manrope and Cormorant Garamond; all fonts are self-hosted
- Headings, prices, copy and controls remain accessible HTML; decorative legal engraving belongs only to the 3D sculpture

## Motion

- Hero parallax, moving Three.js light/refraction and transform-only entrance; headline and artwork are visible before JavaScript hydrates
- Scroll-lit manifesto, readable chapter holds, a Blender/Three.js document sculpture in practices and a genuine Higgsfield video in the process section
- One native scroll position and the existing Lenis owner. No added wheel interception, bounce, flash or abrupt camera motion
- The Blender-authored GLB loads only when its section approaches the viewport, never in the initial hero bundle
- Narrow or short screens use natural document flow. Mobile and low-memory devices omit WebGL; reduced-motion and data-saving also omit video downloads
- GPU drawing and video pause outside their visible section or when the tab is hidden

## What NOT to Do

- No bright neon, saturated aurora gradients or fantasy-cave imagery
- No fast camera moves, flashes, jump cuts or dense particle storms
- No generic stock Arctic landscapes, people, animals or buildings
- No controls, prices, badges or promotional headings baked into artwork
- No mandatory WebGL dependency: static posters and all real content remain usable without it
- No outlined/boxed primary buttons or generic rounded cards on the homepage
